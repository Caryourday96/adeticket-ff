# Game-night quick check

Use https://ff.adeticket.com. Checked against release 83626ca on 11 October 2026.

## Before guests arrive

1. Sign in to the host desk. Open the intended saved game, or create one with the correct teams and question pack. Check the question-mix preview and any question-use limit before creating it.
2. Use a separate Practice game for a quick round and Fast Money rehearsal. Practice does not add question usage. Do not practise by revealing answers in tonight's real game.
3. Open Audience screen and put its public board on the actual TV/display. Check readable text, picture and volume. Use the audience sound test; a connected screen alone does not prove sound works.
4. Expand Event readiness. Confirm picture and audible sound (or intentionally silent mode). On two real phones, join and approve a player for each team. Select **Auto-select face-off captains**, reveal and read the question, then open buzzers and confirm the host sees the winner. Return to the intended starting stage.
5. Keep the host device powered and on reliable Wi-Fi. Keep the host URL private; share the player join link/QR with guests. Lock new joins once everyone needed has joined.

## During play

- Use Pause for interruptions. Review the board before using Undo.
- In Fast Money, board selections or Record & next save answers. Typed drafts alone do not score and clear on reload. End turn & reveal asks for confirmation; cancelling does not pause the timer.
- On a timeout or reconnect, check the latest board/state before repeating an action. A timed-out request may already have applied. Commands are not automatically retried.
- If casting fails, use the public Audience link on a browser/display or a cable connection. Real TV discovery and audio must be checked on your hardware.

## After play

Download the completed scorecard if wanted. Keep saved real games if using the question-use limit: deleting game history can make its questions eligible again. Delete only unwanted Practice games after reviewing the confirmation.

No production restart, saved-game deletion or owner-data edits were performed for this housekeeping pass. Hardware sound/casting and production restart durability remain unverified.

## Reuse teams and phones

On the host setup page, choose a finished real game under **Reuse teams and phones**. Create the new game with the desired question selection. Saved team names/rosters and approved phone registrations are copied; old scores and history stay in the old game. Pending registrations are not approved automatically. Players begin off stage with buzzers closed.

Players keep their existing phone page open and tap **Continue to next game** when it appears (within the usual five-second refresh). The server verifies their old private cookie and the copied registration before setting a cookie for the new room. No credentials are placed in the QR or URL. Refreshing the new phone page retains registration. Use the same browser; cleared or expired cookies (currently seven days) require joining again. Keep the old game until all phones have continued. Repeated creation from the same previous game opens its existing next game.

The audience join QR remains visible during regular rounds, Fast Money and results, including the shared Google Cast audience view. The QR does not bypass joining locks or host approval. Actual camera scanning distance depends on the TV and phone and should be checked at the venue.

## Reveal the question when ready

For each regular round, use **Auto-select face-off captains** to fill open spots with the earliest approved phone on each team. To choose someone else, use **Put [name] on stage**. This changes the player's face-off assignment. The team roster captain still handles steals. The audience board lists both lineups and highlights **Next to answer** during play.

Select **Reveal question** and read the full question aloud. Phones stay locked while you read. Then select **Open buzzers**, which fills any remaining spots using the same join order. Manual buzzer fallback becomes available after opening. The reveal survives a refresh or reconnect, and the next round starts with its question hidden. The higher board rank wins a face-off when two answers are worth the same points.

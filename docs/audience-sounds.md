# Audience sounds

Open a game's audience link, then click **Enable audience sound** on the device connected to your speakers. Use **Test sound**, the volume slider and **Mute audience** before guests arrive. Browsers require this first click; refreshing starts muted again. Keep the host's optional sounds off when the audience speakers are nearby to avoid an echo.

Original synthesized effects accompany buzzes, answer reveals, strikes, round wins and the Fast Money clock. Loading a board, undo, roster edits and catching up after reconnecting do not replay old effects. Timer expiry sounds only when the connected screen observes the clock reach zero.

The Google Cast receiver attempts to enable its own sound. Test your specific TV: device autoplay policies can block audio, in which case the receiver displays a warning and the board keeps working. TV volume/mute are controlled by the TV remote. See [Google Cast setup](google-cast.md) for registration and activation.

These effects are generated locally and are not audio recordings from the TV programme. Apple TV casting is not implemented by the Google Cast integration.

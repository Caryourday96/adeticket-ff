# Monetisation options

Decision recorded: 21 September 2026. Prepare display advertising, disabled until an approved account and consent setup are available.

| Option                 | Offer                                                                   | Best fit                                     |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Pay per event          | Organiser buys a hosting pass; players join free                        | Occasional parties                           |
| Host subscription      | Recurring hosting, custom packs and branding                            | MCs, churches, event planners and businesses |
| Event sponsorship      | Sponsor logo on the lobby, breaks and results                           | Shared audience screen                       |
| Premium question packs | Original surveyed packs about Nigerian music, weddings and other themes | Add-on to hosting                            |
| Display ads            | Google ads on suitable public content pages                             | Secondary revenue as traffic grows           |

## Suggested experiments

- Free demo: short game and starter pack.
- Event pass: test CA$10–20 per event.
- Regular host plan: test CA$25–40 per month.
- Example only: 20 passes at CA$15 = CA$300 gross, before fees and operating costs.

These are proposed test prices, not validated market rates or revenue forecasts. Measure host conversion, repeat usage, support effort and net revenue before expanding. No paid plans, payment collection or sponsorship sales have been implemented by this change.

Recommendation: validate event passes and sponsorships first; keep joining free. Build paid question packs from original questions and collected surveys; do not assume extracted TV material can be resold.

## Advertising boundaries

The first manual ad unit is below the How to play content at `/rules`. No ads on join forms, buzzers, host controls, surveys or live boards. Do not enable Auto ads, overlays, interstitials or automatic refresh: they could interrupt a live event or place ads near interactive controls.

Ads are currently off. See [advertising setup](advertising.md) for activation and verification steps.

References: [AdSense eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724), [gameplay placements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2768340), [consent requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116).
